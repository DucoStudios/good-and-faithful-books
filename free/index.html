What's inside the free pack

18 printable pages for kids 6 to 10, made from the world of Penny and the Golden Notebook. Print the pages you like. Do them together, or let your child explore alone.

  • Two word searches: Penny's money words, and the words that describe a good and faithful builder.
  • A maze through Oak Harbor: help Penny find Cinnamon, the runaway dog.
  • Lemonade stand math: count costs, prices and profit, just like Penny and Emma.
  • Spot the problem: short stories where your child finds the problem and thinks of a way to help. "Problems are gold."
  • My Golden Notebook: a page for your child's own ideas, the way Penny writes everything down.
  • Coloring pages with Penny, Emma and Grandma Rose.
  • 12 family conversation cards about work, giving, saving and kindness, plus an answer key for the grown-ups.

How to use it to teach your child about money

Kids learn about money whether we plan it or not, often from ads and screens. The pack lets your child learn it from a story instead, and from conversations with you.

  1. Start with a puzzle. Kids open up more easily when their hands are busy.
  2. Ask one question from the page, then listen more than you talk.
  3. Keep the conversation cards in a jar on the dinner table and pull one a night.

Questions

Who is the pack for?

Kids around 6 to 10 and the grown-ups who read with them, at home, in church kids' groups or in homeschool. It works even if you haven't read the book yet.

Does it cost anything?

No, the pack is free. You also get a short series of emails with ideas from the books, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

Is it Christian?

Yes. The pack builds on the same values as the books: helping others, being faithful in little things, giving and saving. The story always comes first, never a lecture.
